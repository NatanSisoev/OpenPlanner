# The Efficiency Multiplier

Build something that makes people 10x more effective (developer tools, automation, AI assistance)

Or solving a really hard problem and converting it into a really simple one.

 - Measurable leverage -> demonstrate effectiveness
 - Reusability
 - Platform Thinking -> does this enable others to build on top of it?
 - Enables others
